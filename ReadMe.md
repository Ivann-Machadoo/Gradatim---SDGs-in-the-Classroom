# 🌍 GRADATIM: Engage, Challenge, and Explore the SDGs

### 🚀 Bringing the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) to Life in the Classroom

Gradatim is a **web-based application** designed to engage high school students with the **United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)** in a challenging, interactive, and fun way. This platform fosters **interdisciplinary learning** by encouraging students to explore how **two SDGs** can intersect, creating complex real-world problems. Gradatim provides the tools and guidance for students to build innovative solutions to these challenges.

## 📚 **About the Project**

In 2015, the **United Nations** introduced the **Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs)**, a global framework consisting of 17 interconnected goals aimed at addressing the world’s most pressing challenges. From **poverty**, **education**, and **climate action** to **inequality**, these goals provide a pathway to achieving **sustainable development by 2030**.

Gradatim aims to:
- 📖 Educate high school students on the **importance of the SDGs**.
- 💡 Foster **critical thinking** through real-world challenges.
- 🌍 Encourage **global collaboration** and **interdisciplinary learning**.

By leveraging **interactive digital tools**, such as **challenge-based learning** and **educational games**, we make the SDGs accessible and exciting for students, moving beyond memorization to hands-on problem-solving.

---

## 🔍 **Project Overview**

The **Gradatim web application** serves as an innovative educational platform designed to help high school students **explore and understand** the SDGs through:
1. **Interactive learning** 📱
2. **Challenge-based methodologies** 🧠
3. **Global collaboration** 🌐

Instead of introducing each SDG in isolation, Gradatim **combines two SDGs** to explore how they overlap, creating complex global challenges. This approach encourages students to:
- 🚀 **Think deeply** about the interconnectedness of global issues.
- 🛠️ **Build their own challenges** using real-world data (including NASA's environmental data 🌍).
- 📊 Propose solutions grounded in evidence and **scientific insights**.

---

## 🎮 **Features of Gradatim**

1. **Build Your Challenge** 🛠️: Students develop their own problem scenarios based on the **intersection of two SDGs**, with real-world data from **NASA** integrated into their analysis.
   
2. **Global Board** 🌍: An interactive space for students to share their challenges and solutions with a **global community**, fostering **collaboration** and **peer learning**.

3. **ODIS (On-Demand Interactive Shorts)** 🎬: Short, engaging educational videos, similar to TikToks or reels, provide quick, digestible content about the SDGs.

4. **Games** 🎮: Fun, interactive mini-games designed to reinforce students' understanding of the SDGs in an engaging, dynamic way.

## 💡 **Our Methodology**

By connecting two seemingly unrelated SDGs, students are challenged to develop **higher-order thinking skills** such as:
- **Analysis** 🔍
- **Evaluation** 📊
- **Synthesis** 🧠

This is essential for solving the **multifaceted challenges** of today’s world. The **Gradatim web app** prepares students not only for **academic success** but also for the **complexities of real-world problem-solving**, a critical skill for future careers.

### Why Challenge-Based Learning? 🎓
Research shows that **challenge-based learning** and **problem-solving** promote:
- **Deeper understanding** 📚
- **Long-term retention** 🧠
- **Engagement with complex topics** 🌍

By blending **challenge-based learning** with **gamification**, Gradatim creates an environment where students can:
- Experiment 🎮
- Learn through failure 💡
- Apply **critical thinking** to real-world problems 🌱

---

## 🔧 **Pedagogical Foundations**

Gradatim is grounded in **constructivist learning theory**, emphasizing that students **construct their own understanding** through:
- Inquiry 🤔
- Exploration 🌍
- Reflection 🔄

This approach is especially effective for **high school students**, who are transitioning from concrete to abstract thinking. By focusing on how two SDGs intersect, Gradatim encourages:
- **Problem-solving** 🧠
- **Critical analysis** 🔍
- **Creative solutions** 🛠️
