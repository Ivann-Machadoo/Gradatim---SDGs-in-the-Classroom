SDGs

Why it arose:
The goal aims to eradicate extreme poverty for all people everywhere, currently measured as living on less than $1.90 a day. It emphasizes social protection systems, equal rights to economic resources, and access to basic services to ensure sustainable poverty reduction globally.

Real-world example:
India - Direct transfers to families. During the pandemic, India implemented the Pradhan Mantri Garib Kalyan Yojana, a financial assistance program for over 800 million people in poverty. These direct aids were crucial for families affected by the crisis, ensuring access to food and other essential goods.
