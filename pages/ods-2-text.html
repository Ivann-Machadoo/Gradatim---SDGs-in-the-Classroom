SDGs

Why it arose:
This goal focuses on ending hunger, achieving food security, improving nutrition, and promoting sustainable agriculture. It seeks to double agricultural productivity, ensure access to nutritious food, and promote resilient agricultural practices, especially in regions affected by climate change.

Real-world example:
The Canadian Space Agency (CSA), through projects like growing food in space, is developing technologies to produce healthy food in space and remote areas. This contributes to food security on Earth by improving agricultural practices in challenging environments, supporting SDG 2​.
