Why Gradatim?

In 2015, the United Nations launched the 2030 Sustainable Development Goals (SDG) Agenda to tackle the world's most pressing challenges, from climate change and poverty to pollution and inequality. With 17 goals addressing these areas, we are now halfway through this ambitious agenda. But one major challenge remains: how to engage the youth in actively learning about the SDGs and their importance.

Gradatim responds to this challenge by offering a unique, interactive platform designed specifically for high school classrooms. Students not only learn about the SDGs, but they also discover how these global goals intersect with real-world issues. Our platform integrates NASA’s Earth observation data, providing real-time environmental insights, and allowing students to ground their solutions in scientific evidence.

The challenge for educators is to develop lesson plans that teach the SDGs in the context of existing science curricula. With Gradatim, students can explore how climate change connects to weather patterns, how soil health impacts sustainable agriculture, or how pollution affects biodiversity. This innovative, hands-on approach ensures that students are not just learning—they are engaging with the material in ways that prepare them to become future leaders in solving global challenges.
